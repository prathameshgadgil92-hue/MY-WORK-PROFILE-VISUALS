Firm Overview

Professional attention. Structured execution.

P. V. Gadgil & Co. is a Mumbai-based Chartered Accountancy firm providing assurance, taxation, financial reporting and business advisory services. Assignments are supervised by the proprietor and supported by documented procedures, review records and engagement-specific communication.

The firm serves organisations across manufacturing and service sectors, with assignments spanning statutory and tax audits, consolidation, internal controls, direct and indirect taxes, accounting support and transaction-specific advisory.

Professional Services

Assurance, reporting, tax and compliance

Services are undertaken subject to engagement-specific scope, professional requirements, independence considerations and applicable law.

Statutory Audit

Audits of companies, LLPs and firms; audit planning, risk assessment, substantive and control procedures, reporting and finalisation.

Consolidation

Group reporting support, consolidation procedures, inter-company reconciliations, eliminations and consolidated financial statement audit support.

Tax Audit

Tax audits of companies, LLPs, firms and eligible individuals, including clause-wise review, reconciliations and reporting.

Internal Audit

Risk-based review of processes, internal controls, compliance, transaction cycles and management action points.

Internal Financial Controls

Design and operating-effectiveness review, control documentation, walkthroughs, testing and deficiency reporting.

Stock & Process Audit

Physical verification, inventory controls, ageing and valuation review, stock records, process compliance and exception reporting.

Financial Statements

Preparation, review and finalisation of standalone and consolidated financial statements, schedules and disclosures.

Direct Tax and TDS/TCS

Income-tax returns, computations, applicability review, withholding compliance, reconciliations, corrections and transaction-specific advice.

GST

Registration, return filing, ITC review, reconciliations, refund support, advisory and departmental proceedings.

Entity Compliance

Accounting, tax and regulatory support for LLPs, partnership firms and co-operative housing societies.

Accounting Advisory

Accounting treatment, policies, estimates, disclosures, transaction analysis and implementation support.
